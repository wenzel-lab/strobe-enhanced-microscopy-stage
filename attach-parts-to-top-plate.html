Attach parts to the top plate

Tools

  • 1 2.5mm Ball-end Allen key
  • 1 Soldering iron

Laser cut Parts

  • 1 Top plate

Mechanical Components

  • 6 Heat insert
  • 4 M3x10mm cap head screw
  • 2 M3x8mm cap head screw

Printed Parts

  • 4 Standoff-M

Sub-Assemblies

  • 1 focusing mechanism

You will also need the parts you assembled in previous sections.

Step 1: Prepare the top plate

  • Positionate a heat insert in each corner of the top plate.
  • Apply heat to each insert (using a soldering iron) and use gentle force to push it into position, as described in the guide to use heat-set inserts.
  • Repeat this step to put two more inserts in the plate to attach the focusing assembly.

Step 2: Prepare the standoffs

  • Insert a M3x10mm cap head screw into each standoff using the large hole that comes out the side of them. The screw should poke out the end of the part as shown.
  • Use a 2.5mm ball-ended hex key to turn the screw.

Step 3: Attach the standoffs

  • Attach each standoff to one of the corners of the top plate.

Step 4: Attach the focusing assembly

  • Use a M3x8mm cap head screw on either side of the focusing mechanism, attach it to the top plate.
